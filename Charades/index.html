Charades

Play with friends and family. Spend quality time together playing Charades, best party and family game. The game is full of joy and new experiences. Develop you communication and motor skills while playing.

Guess the words on the phone based on the hints of your friends. Talk, act it out, sing, dance, mime the words.

Suitable for all age groups and contains engaging decks with cards to satisfy various interests.

How to play Charades:

  1. Choose one of the many decks available.
  2. Hold your phone on your forehead.
  3. Try to guess the word that is on the phone using hints from your friends who see the word on the phone.
  4. If you guessed correctly press anywhere on the right side of the screen. If you want to skip the words you simply tap the left part of the screen.
  5. Guess as much words as possible while timer is active.
  6. Stop when the timer runs out and check how many words you guessed.
  7. Pass the phone to your friends, so he also can show off.

Have immense fun!
